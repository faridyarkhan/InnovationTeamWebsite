Privacy Policy

How we handle your information.

Effective Date: November 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Innovantage IT Consultancies India Private Limited (“Innovantage India”, “we”, “us”, or “our”), acting as a Data Fiduciary under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDPA) and applicable data protection laws, handles personal data collected through this website.

1. Personal Data We Collect

We only collect personal data that you voluntarily submit when contacting us via our Contact Us form, email, or telephone:

  • Full name
  • Business or personal email address
  • Phone number (optional)
  • The contents of your inquiry or message

2. Purpose & Lawful Basis of Processing

We process your personal data strictly on the basis of your consent and legitimate business communication purposes:

  • To respond to your inquiries, schedule discovery calls, and evaluate potential engagements
  • To maintain business correspondence and comply with applicable legal or regulatory obligations

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data, nor do we use it for automated profiling or third-party advertising.

3. Cookies, Browser Storage & Tracking

This website itself does not set tracking cookies, advertising pixels, third-party analytics scripts, or persistent browser storage (such as localStorage). When you visit our Contact Us page, the embedded Microsoft Form (forms.cloud.microsoft) may set strictly necessary functional cookies within its frame to deliver and protect the form. Standard web-server and CDN infrastructure logs (such as IP address, timestamp, and requested URL) may also be processed temporarily by our hosting provider solely for security, DDoS mitigation, and service reliability.

4. Data Sharing & Cloud Processing

Inquiries submitted through our embedded contact form are collected and transmitted over encrypted HTTPS via Microsoft Forms (Microsoft 365) solely to route and respond to your request. Access is restricted to authorized personnel on a need-to-know basis, and data processors are bound by confidentiality and security obligations.

5. Data Retention & Security

We retain contact inquiry records only for as long as necessary to fulfill your request, manage an active business relationship, or meet statutory retention requirements, after which they are securely deleted. We apply appropriate technical and organizational safeguards—including TLS encryption in transit, access controls, and strict browser security policies—to protect your data against unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure.

6. Your Rights as a Data Principal

Subject to applicable law (including the DPDPA 2023), you have the right to:

  • Request a summary of the personal data we hold about you and how it is processed
  • Request correction, completion, updating, or erasure of your personal data
  • Withdraw your consent to further processing at any time
  • Seek grievance redressal or nominate an individual to exercise your rights in the event of incapacity or death

7. Grievance Officer & Privacy Contact

To exercise your rights, withdraw consent, or raise any privacy concern, please contact our Grievance Officer:
